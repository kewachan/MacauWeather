Privacy Policy

Your data stays minimal.

Macau Weather is designed to provide weather information without accounts or advertising tracking.

Information we use

The app may use device location, if allowed, to find the nearest Macau observation station. Location is used for app functionality and is not sold.

Notifications

The app may use push notification tokens to deliver weather warning notifications selected by the user.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app does not receive your payment card details.

Contact

For privacy questions, email kevin.kw.chan@icloud.com.
