Warnings

Local conditions

Screenshots

Preview the main Now page, official warning status and weather-measure guidance in the app.

What you can check

  • Real-time weather from the nearest available observation station.
  • Seven-day forecast, 48-hour forecast charts, UV index, air quality and bridge wind.
  • Rainfall amount, water level monitoring, tide information, radar, lightning and satellite images.
  • Official warning status and warning probabilities when available.
  • Home Screen widgets and tropical cyclone Live Activity support.

Designed for daily use

The app keeps the main screen focused on current conditions and lets you customize which Now cards are shown. Warnings and weather measures are separated so urgent information is easier to scan.

Weather data may change quickly. Always follow the latest official announcements and government instructions during severe weather.
